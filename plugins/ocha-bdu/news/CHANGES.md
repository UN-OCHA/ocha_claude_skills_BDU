# What's new in the OCHA BDU skills

Newest first. Written for colleagues, in plain words.

## 2026.11.26.3

- Connecting Claude to the brand site: Claude now finds the frontify-mcp folder wherever your Dropbox keeps it. If it still can’t, it asks you to make that folder available offline and drag it into the chat.

## 2026.11.26.2

- Logo and template links on the brand site now use Dropbox’s download version, so one click downloads the file instead of opening a Dropbox preview.

## 2026.11.26

- The Frontify skill now explains BDU’s own blocks on the brand site (Font preview, AI prompt, Useful links): what each is for and how to add one, for example a new font.
- Logos and templates on the brand site are Dropbox links, not uploads, so updating the file in Dropbox updates the page. Claude makes the link and offers to connect your Dropbox first if it isn’t yet.

## 2026.11.25.3

- **Three views of funding (data):** Claude now keeps all humanitarian funding, the Global Humanitarian Overview (GHO) plans and the prioritized GHO apart, and says which one each figure comes from.
- **More funding tables:** you can ask for prioritized figures by sector, plan, donor or recipient organization, and Claude can search the whole FTS dashboard for a table before saying it isn’t there.
- **JPEG finals at full quality:** final JPEGs, including mockups, are now always exported at quality 100.

## 2026.11.25.2

- **Editable Illustrator files now look like the final:** textures, torn edges and shadows come in as images, one per piece, so they can still be moved.
- **Layers by meaning:** the .ai is organised into one layer per part of the design (background, heading, each section), each with its own text, shapes and images.
- **Layout tips in the graphic styles:** organise content in blocks with their own space, keep related things together, and let arrows leave a group from its middle.

## 2026.11.25

- **Large numbers (charts, visual identity, editorial style):** Claude now writes 1.2 million and $4.5 billion in full when there’s room, and keeps 1.2M and $4.5B for tight spaces like chart labels. Below a million it writes 600,400, never 600.4 thousand, and it always rounds in public-facing work.

## 2026.11.24.2

- **New: graphic styles.** BDU’s named looks now have their own skill, for static graphics as well as films. Ask for “the newsprint style” or “the cut-out style” and Claude follows the recipe.
- **Newsprint**, the first new style: cream printed paper, OCHA icons in ink, black caption strips and an orange marker on the key idea. A sober, newsy look for explainers and diagrams, with a starter graphic to copy.
- **Old pictures for free:** the style points to the Public Domain Image Archive for photos, old charts and engravings that need no credit.

## 2026.11.24

- **Updates from Javi:** update notes now say plainly when Javi recommends a step, and Claude offers to do it for you.
- **Staying up to date:** if your OCHA skills get stuck on an old version, Claude now notices and tells you exactly what to do.
- **Javi recommends:** if you still have old OCHA skill folders whose names don’t start with “ocha-bdu-” (from before Monday’s rename), let Claude delete them. They are out of date and make Claude load every skill twice.

## 2026.11.23.8

- All OCHA BDU skills now start with ocha-bdu- (for example ocha-bdu-visual-identity, ocha-bdu-video, ocha-bdu-mapping). They work the same; only the names changed.
- If one of your project notes still uses an old name (like ocha-video), ask Claude to update it to the new one.

## 2026.11.23.7

- Dropbox links: the first time you mention sharing a Dropbox file, Claude explains what it can do (links that open for anyone, checked before you get them) and offers a 5-minute setup.

## 2026.11.23.6

- The skills now say BDU instead of the old DMU when they mention the team Dropbox. Folder locations on your computer are unchanged.

## 2026.11.23.5

- Video review tool: the play and comment buttons now use the free Font Awesome icons. They look slightly bolder.
- Skills that point to files in the BDU Dropbox now say these are BDU internal content, with ochavisual@un.org as the contact.

## 2026.11.23.4

- Public Dropbox links: small wording update to the examples in the setup steps. Nothing changes in how you use it.

## 2026.11.23.3

- **Public Dropbox links (ocha-dropbox-links, new):** Claude can now make Dropbox links that open for anyone, not only the OCHA team, and check whether a link asks people to sign in. The first time, Claude walks you through a 5-minute setup that connects your own Dropbox.
- **Logos (ocha-logo-production):** no more making public links by hand in Dropbox: Claude makes them with the new Dropbox skill.

## 2026.11.23.2

- **Logos (ocha-logo-production):** clearer advice for sharing logo files from Dropbox: make the link by hand in Dropbox (Share → “Anyone with the link”) and test it in a private window, because the link Claude’s Dropbox connector makes only opens for OCHA team members.
- **Charts, maps, brand and design (ocha-dataviz, ocha-mapping, ocha-visual-identity, ocha-design):** removed pointers to internal BDU tools you don’t have installed.

## 2026.11.23

- **Photos (ocha-photo):** a new point on sensitive photos: a recognisable woman at the door of her own home can be identified and found, however beautiful the photo. Weigh it case by case, and when in doubt pick people seen from behind or at a distance, or let the shelters and objects tell the story.
- **Social media (ocha-social-media):** Claude now uses the latest cleared copy from the Trello comments, can bold just a few words in a text box, and accepts a manual line break when no box width avoids a short last line.

## 2026.11.21

- **Social media (ocha-social-media):** Claude now does the Canva steps that need Chrome (fonts, guides, transitions) straight away, without stopping to ask each time.
- **Social media (ocha-social-media):** new layout tips: crop a wide group photo to one clear subject on 3:4 slides, choose photos that show what the figure says, and use a soft shade behind small labels on busy photos.
- **Photos (ocha-photo):** searching MediaValet by country works best with the country’s name, not its three-letter code.

## 2026.11.20.2

- **Photos (ocha-photo):** downloading from MediaValet now works on Windows too, and photographer names in any alphabet are kept correctly in the credits.

## 2026.11.20

- **Brand site (ocha-frontify):** Claude now stops before putting one uploaded image on two blocks, so deleting a test section can no longer blank an image on a live page.

## 2026.11.19.4

- **Social media:** new layout tips from Javier: put text in the photo’s empty spaces, keep it well clear of faces in portraits, let the gradient follow the text, and place the photo credit in a bottom corner on the margin.
- **Social media:** to make a country name stand out, set it one weight heavier than the rest of its line.

## 2026.11.19.3

- **Photos:** searching MediaValet by country (“find photos of Burkina Faso…”) works again. It had stopped returning results.

## 2026.11.19.2

- **Brand site (ocha-frontify):** links Claude adds to brand.unocha.org now always use the page’s permanent link, so they keep working when a page is renamed or moved.

## 2026.11.19

- **Funding data (ocha-data):** ask Claude for the link to the FTS funding dashboard, opened straight on any page, such as the Ebola 2026 one-pager.
- **Compress a PDF (ocha-utilities):** it now also shrinks PDFs with see-through images (common in Canva exports) and keeps long A4 reports sharp.

## 2026.11.18.3

- **Compress a PDF (ocha-utilities):** compressing a heavy Canva PDF is now much faster, with the same sharp photos and the same colours as before.

## 2026.11.18.2

- **Funding data (ocha-data):** reading the FTS funding dashboard works again after the FTS team changed it, and Claude can now also read the dashboard’s hidden emergency pages, such as Ebola 2026.
- **Social media (ocha-social-media):** figure slides can have a smaller intro line under the big number, and Claude keeps people in photos out of the dark text gradient.
- **Video review (ocha-video):** the review page now plays at 1.5× and 2× speed.
- **New:** after each update, Claude tells you in a few lines what changed, like this.
