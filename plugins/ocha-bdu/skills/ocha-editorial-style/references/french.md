# French: OCHA products

From the HNRP 2027 French templates (Javier’s decisions, 3 November 2026, unless noted). Terms for one
product belong in that product’s notes; only general ones go here.

## Numbers and units

| English | French |
|---|---|
| 1.5M | 1,5 M |
| $1.5B | 1,5 Md $ |
| $25M | 25 M $ |
| 25% | 25 % |

- Explain abbreviations once, where a page needs it: “M : million / Md : milliard”.
- Use a non-breaking space inside these figures and before `:` `;` `?` `!` `%`.

## Capitals

Keep accents on capitals: RÉPONSE, not REPONSE. French Word drops them on automatic all-caps, so type real
capitals there.

## Terms

| English | French | Note |
|---|---|---|
| affected (people, places) | **affecté(e)s** | Not “touché(e)s”: 220 to 20 in French OCHA plans on ReliefWeb |
| Central Emergency Response Fund | **Fonds central d’intervention d’urgence** | CERF |
| requirements (funding) | **besoins financiers** | Not “exigences” |
