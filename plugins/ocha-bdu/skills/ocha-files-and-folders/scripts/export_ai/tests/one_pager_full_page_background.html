What is Humanitarian Networks and Partnerships Week (HNPW)?

HNPW is an annual gathering that brings together the full spectrum of humanitarian actors, including UN agencies, NGOs, the Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement, governments, academia and private sector. HNPW hosts over 300 sessions organized by over 70 humanitarian networks both online and in-person, facilitating an inter-network platform to exchange knowledge, share experiences, identify innovative solutions, and strengthen partnerships.

What makes HNPW unique

Sessions are led and shaped by practitioners themselves. HNPW prioritizes a bottom-up space for networks to shape the event programme, bring diverse perspectives to the table and influence discussions on the future of humanitarian action.
